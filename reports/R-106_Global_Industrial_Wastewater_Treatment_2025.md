## Industry Dynamics
Zero Liquid Discharge (ZLD) mandates and strict limits on chemical oxygen demand (COD) across manufacturing sectors (pharmaceuticals, chemicals, semiconductor fabrication, and textiles) are driving adoption of membrane bioreactors (MBR) and advanced oxidation processes (AOP).

## Market Sizing & Regional Breakdown
The global industrial wastewater treatment technology market was pegged at USD 15.30 billion in 2024. The sector is expected to register a steady CAGR of 6.8% from 2025 to 2030, reaching USD 21.25 billion by 2030.

### Regional Shares:
- **Asia-Pacific:** 44% (USD 6.73 billion in 2024), driven by extensive manufacturing hubs in China and India.
- **North America:** 26% (USD 3.98 billion in 2024).
- **Europe:** 21% (USD 3.21 billion in 2024).
- **Rest of the World:** 9% (USD 1.38 billion in 2024).
