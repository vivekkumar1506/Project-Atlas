## Overview
Grid reliability and wildfire prevention in the western United States and Canada have made intelligent line sensors, transformer monitoring devices, and synchrophasor PMUs indispensable for investor-owned utilities (IOUs) and regional transmission organizations.

## Valuation & Projections
The North American smart grid sensors market achieved a valuation of USD 2.15 billion in 2024. Fueled by power grid resilience investments and distributed energy resource (DER) integration, the market is anticipated to expand at a CAGR of 12.3% from 2024 to 2030, arriving at USD 4.31 billion by 2030.
