## Market Overview
The global residential water purifier market continues to experience robust consumer adoption driven by heightening health awareness, industrial effluent runoff into groundwater reservoirs, and the proliferation of compact Point-of-Use (POU) reverse osmosis (RO) systems. This market covers under-the-sink, countertop, and whole-house filtration units.

## Market Valuation and Growth Trajectory
The global residential water purifier market was valued at USD 9.54 billion in 2024. Over the forecast horizon spanning 2025 to 2030, the market is projected to expand at a compound annual growth rate (CAGR) of 7.4%, culminating in a global market size of USD 14.61 billion by 2030.

- **Rest of the World:** Comprises 8% of 2024 revenue.
