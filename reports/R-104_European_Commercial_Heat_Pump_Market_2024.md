## Executive Overview
The European transition away from fossil fuel boilers in commercial, institutional, and district heating plants has catalyzed unprecedented demand for high-capacity air-to-water and ground-source commercial heat pumps. Policy frameworks including REPowerEU and the EU F-gas Regulation revision underpin market expansion.

## Market Sizing & Projections
The European commercial heat pump market stood at USD 6.80 billion in 2024. Driven by decarbonization mandates and industrial subsidies, the market is forecast to grow at an accelerating CAGR of 14.1% from 2024 to 2030, reaching USD 15.01 billion by 2030.

### Country Segmentation:
- **Germany:** 26% market share
- **France:** 22% market share
- **Nordic Region (Sweden, Norway, Finland):** 19% market share
- **Rest of Europe:** 33% market share
